Qualifications & Background

A Career Built on Care & Excellence

Dr. Gichina Mukuria is a highly qualified Specialist Dermatologist with advanced training from leading institutions in Kenya, the United Kingdom, and India. He treats patients of all ages — from infants presenting with eczema to adults seeking cosmetic dermatology — with a warm, thorough, and evidence-based approach.

Academic Qualifications
MBChB — Basic Degree in Medicine & Surgery University of Nairobi
MMed (Paeds) — Master in Paediatrics & Child Health University of Nairobi
MSc Clinical Dermatology King's College London, United Kingdom
Fellowship in Aesthetic Medicine ILAMED, New Delhi, India
Practice & Affiliations
Dermatologist — Gertrude's Children's Hospital Muthaiga Road, Nairobi

Member, Kenya Association of Dermatologists
Philosophy of Care

"I believe in listening carefully, examining thoroughly, and explaining clearly. Whether it's a persistent rash, a mole check, or a cosmetic concern, my goal is to make you feel understood and to give you the best possible outcome for your skin."
