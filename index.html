About the Doctor

Dr. Benard Gichina
Mukuria

Dr. Benard Gichina Mukuria is a Pediatric Dermatologist practicing at Gertrude's Children's Hospital — East Africa's premier dedicated children's hospital — as well as at Landmark Plaza, Argwings Kodhek Road, Nairobi.

He specialises exclusively in dermatological conditions affecting infants, children, and adolescents. From newborn rashes and childhood eczema to teenage acne and complex inflammatory skin disorders, Dr. Mukuria combines clinical expertise with a gentle, child-centred approach that puts both child and parent at ease.

With a 4.9-star rating from verified Nairobi parents, Dr. Mukuria's practice is built on thorough diagnosis, clear communication, and treatment plans that genuinely work for each child's unique skin type and condition.

What We Treat

Pediatric Skin
Services

Book Now
🧒
Childhood Eczema

Personalised management of atopic dermatitis — trigger identification, treatment plans, and long-term flare prevention for all ages.

🌿
Adolescent Acne

Age-appropriate treatment for developing skin and melanin-rich complexions — both medical and cosmetic approaches available.

🔬
Hyperpigmentation

Treatment of dark spots and uneven skin tone using therapies specifically safe and effective for children's sensitive skin.

🛡️
Skin Infections

Diagnosis and treatment of bacterial, viral, and fungal infections — ringworm, impetigo, warts, molluscum, and more.

✨
Birthmarks & Haemangiomas

Expert evaluation of birthmarks, port-wine stains, and infantile haemangiomas — with referral for laser therapy when indicated.

🔍
Psoriasis & Vitiligo

Evidence-based management of psoriasis, vitiligo, and complex paediatric skin conditions using child-safe protocols.
